02 · The chronicle

The story so far

No overnight glow-up, just steady progress.

DEC 2025 · CHAPTER 1

I had stories to tell

I felt I had stories worth telling, so I started documenting my life. The response was better than I expected.

CHAPTER 2

Cracked an IIT internship, then helped others do it too

I realised I'm good at studies, and I landed a research internship at IIT Roorkee. Then I started helping people in college do the same. It worked, and I kept going.

CHAPTER 3

Started running

I took up running, and with it came events, medals and a lot of new people.

NOW · CHAPTER 4

Fat to fit, with acads on track

Now I try to inspire people to go from fat to fit while balancing academics at the same time.
